Turning Community Needs into Practical Action

BHB Foundation works across several interconnected areas of development.

Our activities may include:
  • Primary healthcare support
  • Maternal and child health initiatives
  • Health awareness campaigns
  • Community health outreaches
  • Nutrition awareness
  • Referrals and community linkages

Our Community Development Approach

How We Create Change: A structured 5-step methodology ensuring verified outcomes, relevance, and lasting ownership.

01

Listen

We engage communities and identify their priorities, challenges and opportunities.

02

Assess

We assess identified needs and determine where our resources and partnerships can make a meaningful contribution.

03

Empower

We provide appropriate support, skills, resources and opportunities based on the needs of the programme.

04

Connect

We connect individuals and communities with relevant institutions, services, partners and opportunities.

05

Follow Up

We monitor programme activities, document progress and gather feedback to understand results and improve future interventions.

Active Partners, Not Passive Recipients

View Active Projects →

We believe that enduring transformation occurs only when communities actively participate in designing and managing their own solutions.

Every BHB initiative is co-designed with local leaders, verified through household assessments, and handed over to self-governing community committees equipped to sustain progress independently.
