What We Do

Six strategic pillars designed to address systemic poverty, health inequities, and social barriers across Northern Nigeria.

Strategic Pillars of Sustainable Change

Grounded in verified grassroots assessments and implemented with community ownership.

Pillar 01 · Gender Dignity & Health

Every Girl Deserves Dignity: Menstrual Hygiene Awareness and Sanitary Pad Distribution

We educate and empower girls in secondary schools on menstrual health and hygiene, while providing sanitary pads and essential hygiene supplies. Our goal is to ensure that every girl can manage her period safely, comfortably, and with dignity — without shame, stigma, or missing school.

Explore Associated Projects →
Pillar 02 · WASH & Infrastructure

Safe Water, Safer Communities: Clean Water Infrastructure & Hygiene Promotion

We construct and rehabilitate solar-powered community boreholes, establish community water management committees, and deliver WASH education to eliminate waterborne disease outbreaks in rural settlements.

Explore Associated Projects →
Pillar 03 · Child Welfare

Protecting the Future: Nutrition, Health, and Social Protection for Vulnerable Children

Delivering community-based acute malnutrition screenings, emergency nutritional supplements, and educational enrollment toolkits for orphans and vulnerable children across peri-urban settlements.

Explore Associated Projects →
Pillar 04 · Women Livelihoods

Empowering Widows and Vulnerable Women Through Agro-Enterprise Support

Providing direct seed capital, post-harvest agro-processing machinery, cooperative bookkeeping training, and direct market access linkages to widowed mothers and female-headed households.

Explore Associated Projects →
Pillar 05 · Digital Inclusion

Inclusive Tech for Girls with Disabilities

Delivering intensive digital literacy boot camps, assistive screen-reader computing, and hardware grants for young women living with visual and physical impairments across Kano State.

Explore Associated Projects →
Pillar 06 · Youth Mentorship

Youth Skills Development & Entrepreneurship Support

Vocational apprenticeships in renewable solar installation, electrical repairs, tailoring, and digital entrepreneurship equipped with starter equipment toolkits.

Explore Associated Projects →

Community-Led Sustainability

Active Partners, Not Passive Recipients

We believe that lasting development happens when communities are active participants in solving their own challenges.

Every BHB project is designed with local input, managed with local oversight, and handed over to community committees equipped to maintain progress long after initial funding.
