About BHB Foundation

An incorporated non-governmental organisation advancing welfare, healthcare access, and economic self-reliance across underserved communities.

Our Story

Supporting families and communities facing social and economic challenges.

BHB Foundation was established from a commitment to supporting families and communities facing social and economic challenges.

Our work is rooted in the understanding that many communities have the capacity and determination to improve their circumstances but may lack access to the resources, opportunities and institutional support required to do so.

We therefore work alongside communities rather than simply working for them.

Through targeted programmes, partnerships and community engagement, we aim to contribute to healthier, more inclusive and economically resilient communities.
