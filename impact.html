Strategic SDG Alignment

Sustainable Development Goals Matrix

How BHB interventions map directly into the United Nations Global Sustainable Development Goals.

SDG 3 · Good Health

Health & Social Well-being

Preventative maternal screenings, health education, and mobile medical posts in hard-to-reach settlements.

SDG 4 · Quality Education

Inclusive Digital Skills

Accessible screen-reading software, assistive coding boot camps for girls with disabilities, and school re-enrollment support.

SDG 5 · Gender Equality

Women & Female Heads of Household

Micro-seed capital, tailoring equipment, grain processing machinery, and financial literacy circles.

SDG 8 · Decent Work

Youth Technical Livelihoods

Vocational training, electronics and IT repairs, and structured trade apprenticeships.

SDG 10 · Reduced Inequalities

Disability Tech Inclusion

Breaking socio-economic exclusion by equipping marginalized persons with marketable digital skills.

SDG 11 · Resilient Communities

Grassroots Leadership

Community-led Water Management Committees and disaster resilience frameworks.

Empowering Families. Strengthening Communities. Creating Sustainable Futures across Kano State, Nigeria.
