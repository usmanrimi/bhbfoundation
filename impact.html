How We Measure & Verify Impact

Every project undertaken by BHB Foundation follows strict on-ground verification protocols to guarantee direct community benefits.

01

Baseline Assessment

Conducting in-person household surveys to identify vulnerabilities and establish verifiable baseline indicators.

02

Audited Allocation

Disbursing project funds in controlled milestone tranches tied to verified project execution.

03

Direct Delivery

Supplies, assistive tools, and medical kits are handed directly to verified individual beneficiaries.

04

Longitudinal Review

Follow-up assessments at 3, 6, and 12 months to verify business survival, health status, and community ownership.
