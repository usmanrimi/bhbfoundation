Our Commitment to Responsible Development

We believe that trust is built through responsible management, documentation and transparency.

Where appropriate, programme information and impact reports will be made available to stakeholders.

We therefore seek to maintain appropriate records relating to:

  • Programme activities
  • Beneficiaries
  • Project expenditure
  • Donations and grants
  • Procurement
  • Field activities
  • Monitoring and evaluation
  • Partner contributions

Our Impact Framework

A structured methodology tracking inputs, activities, outputs, outcomes, and lasting systemic transformation.

Inputs
