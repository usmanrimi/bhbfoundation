How We Measure & Verify Real Impact

Every project undertaken by BHB Foundation follows strict on-ground verification protocols to guarantee direct, measurable community benefits.

1

Baseline Assessment

Conducting in-person household surveys to identify vulnerabilities and set measurable baselines.

2

Audited Allocation

Disbursing funds exclusively in controlled tranches. Next-phase grants require audited progress.

3

Post-Delivery Verification

Independent officers confirm that supplies and clinics match required operational standards.

4

Ongoing Monitoring

Longitudinal reviews at 3 and 6 months to verify skills adoption and sustainable family welfare.
