Turning Assistive Technology into Lasting Independence

“Before this boot camp, I believed computer programming was closed to someone who is blind. Today, I built my own digital profile and learned how software can empower everyone.”
— Amina S., Age 17, Digital Skills Cohort Fellow

Funded directly by BHB Foundation in partnership with The Ability First Tech Hub, this 120-hour intensive program provided accessible screen-reader laptops, accessible web design fundamentals, and career mentorship.

How We Measure & Verify Real Impact

Every project undertaken by BHB Foundation follows strict on-ground verification protocols to guarantee direct, measurable community benefits.

01
Field Baseline Protocol

Direct Community Assessments

Before launching any project, our field mobilizers conduct in-person household surveys across participating Kano communities to identify acute vulnerabilities and establish measurable baseline milestones.

✓ Community Baseline Surveyed
02
Transparent Stewardship

Accountable Resource Delivery

Every naira received and deployed is accounted for with detailed milestone tracking, transparent vendor audits, and direct in-person handovers to verified beneficiaries and local partner institutions.

✓ 100% Audited Allocation
03
Long-Term Sustainability

Post-Deployment Monitoring

Our commitment extends beyond initial delivery. We conduct 3-month and 6-month longitudinal reviews to verify ongoing skills adoption, health improvements, and sustainable family welfare.

Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Alignment

How every BHB intervention maps directly to measurable international development benchmarks.

SDG 03 · Good Health

Health & Maternal Well-being

Preventative maternal screenings, mobile ultrasound clinics, and newborn hygiene kits in hard-to-reach settlements.

SDG 04 · Quality Education

Inclusive Tech & Literacy

Accessible screen-reading software, assistive coding boot camps for girls with disabilities, and school re-enrollment support.

SDG 05 · Gender Equality

Women & Widows Economic Fund

Direct micro-seed capital, agro-processing machinery, and cooperative financial literacy circles for widowed mothers.

SDG 08 · Decent Work

Youth Technical Livelihoods

Vocational training, solar power system installation, IT hardware diagnostics, and startup business kits.

SDG 10 · Reduced Inequalities

Disability Tech Inclusion

Overcoming socio-economic marginalization by empowering persons with disabilities with high-value digital capabilities.

SDG 11 · Resilient Communities

Grassroots Leadership & WASH

Community Water Management Committees, solar-powered boreholes, and civic infrastructure resilience.
