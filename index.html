Every Individual Deserves A
Healthy & Dignified Life

BHB Foundation is an incorporated non-governmental organization established to advance welfare and self-reliance, expand maternal and primary health access, and equip youths and persons with disabilities with sustainable livelihood tools across Kano State.

“People are not defined by their circumstances. With targeted assistance, inclusive digital tools, and community solidarity, families can build lasting resilience and shape their own destinies.”
Our Mission
Direct Grassroots Action

Walking alongside vulnerable families to expand access to primary health, digital literacy, and economic seed grants.

• Primary Care & Livelihoods
Our Vision
Inclusive Community Dignity

Building self-reliant, resilient communities where every individual lives with dignity, security, and equal opportunity.

• Universal Human Dignity
