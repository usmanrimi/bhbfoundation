Who We Are

Every individual deserves the chance to live a healthy, safe, and dignified life.

BHB Family Support and Development Foundation is a registered not-for-profit non-governmental organization (CAC Reg. No. 9670692) established in July 2026 to advance the welfare, empowerment, and self-reliance of individuals and underserved communities.

“People are not defined by their circumstances. With the right support, opportunities, and partnerships, communities can build resilience and shape their own destinies.”

Our Mission

To walk alongside vulnerable and underserved individuals by improving access to essential health, education, and economic opportunities.

Our Vision

To build inclusive, resilient, and empowered communities where every individual can live with dignity and realize their full potential.
